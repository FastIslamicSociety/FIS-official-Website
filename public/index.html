About FIS
FAST Islamic Society · est. 2025

A student-led society at FAST that aims to show that Islam and university life can go hand in hand.

Founded in 2025, FAST Islamic Society (FIS), formerly known as FAST Quran and Sunnah Society (FQSS), was established with the hope of nurturing a deeper connection with Islam and, above all, with Allah SWT.

Our purpose

We believe that our purpose extends far beyond simply going through life. As the khalifah of Allah SWT on this earth, we have been blessed with abilities, opportunities, and potential that come with a responsibility to use them meaningfully. Our ambitions, knowledge, relationships, and everyday pursuits can all become means of earning His pleasure when rooted in the right intentions and guided by His teachings.

What shapes FIS

This understanding is what shapes FIS. Whether it is a gathering, a trip, a speaker session, or even a simple social media post, we strive to bring our values into everything we do. We recognise Hudoodullah (حدودُ اللہ) as divine boundaries that guide and protect us, helping us distinguish not just between what is permissible and impermissible, but between what brings us closer to Allah and what takes us away from Him.

Faith & dunya

Through FIS, we hope to show that one does not have to compromise on their deen to embrace the joys of this dunya. We can pursue excellence, explore our potential, build friendships, and make beautiful memories, all while keeping our connection with Allah SWT at the centre of it all. Because Islam is not simply a part of the life we live. It is the purpose, guidance, and meaning behind it.
